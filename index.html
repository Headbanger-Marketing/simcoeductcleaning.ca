FAQs

Straight Answers, No Guesswork

Pricing, certification, timing, and the air itself, covered below.

How often should a Simcoe home have the ducts cleaned?

Plan on two years rather than three in this stretch of the county, simply because the sandy soil is always working its way in. Homes ringed by worked fields, with pets, or with a renovation in recent memory should not wait even that long.

Are your technicians certified and insured?

Certified, trained, and insured on every job, with the documents available when you ask. Ontario treats duct cleaning as an unregulated trade, which makes certification and insurance the meaningful checks, and we pass both gladly.

Can you prove the system was actually dirty and actually cleaned?

Scope footage of the trunk lines at the start and again at the end, watched together before we load out. Around Simcoe, most homeowners can predict the before footage; the after is what they book us for again.

Our dryer keeps shutting off mid-cycle. Vent or machine?

Overheating shut-offs usually mean the machine cannot exhale, which points at the vent. We brush the full line, clear the hood, meter the flow, and if the dryer itself is the fault we will tell you plainly rather than sell a second clearing.

What does a visit look like and how long does it take?

Rig connected at the furnace, registers removed and washed, each branch brushed under suction, blower compartment cleaned, footage captured. Most Simcoe homes take three to four hours; sprawling rural properties with long trunk runs take more.

There is a smell when the heat first comes on. Fixable?

Generally yes. The odour rides on residue left from idle, damp months or older occupancy, and it lifts with cleaning. A sanitizer treatment on the freshly cleaned walls handles the stubborn remainder.

Why does the grit come back so fast after we clean?

Because the system is likely ingesting its own basement, open return joints and unsealed takeoffs acting as intakes for sandy air. Sealing those leaks is the companion job that makes a cleaning last.

How far do you travel for jobs?

All of Simcoe plus Port Dover, Delhi, Waterford, Courtland, Langton, and Port Rowan. Shore-bound bookings are easiest scheduled shoulder to shoulder, so ask when you call.

Do you serve areas outside of Simcoe, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Simcoe and nearby communities including Port Dover, Delhi, Waterford, Courtland, and the surrounding area.
